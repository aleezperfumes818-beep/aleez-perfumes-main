# 🌴 Tamil Island Clubbing & Karaoke

A complete multiplayer social hangout experience featuring a **250 × 250 stud tropical island**, modern **Tamil Nightclub**, and interactive **Karaoke Stage** with synchronized lyrics and singing queue.

---

## Features
- **250x250 Tropical Island:** Sandy beach ring, ocean water, palm trees, beach bonfire with log seating, tiki torches, beach loungers & umbrellas.
- **Ocean Boundary Safety:** Teleports players back onto the warm beach if they swim beyond 145 studs or fall into deep water.
- **Central Tamil Nightclub:** Slate & glass pavilion, neon cyan & pink runners, elevated DJ booth with subwoofers, indoor 4x4 LED dance floor.
- **Outdoor Ocean Dance Floor:** 3x3 LED dance floor on a wooden boardwalk facing the open ocean.
- **Interactive Karaoke Stage:**
  - Large 3D Lyrics Screen displaying live synchronized song lyrics line-by-line.
  - Interactive Singing Queue with stage teleport & handheld microphone tool.
  - Performance rewards (+20 Vibes) and automatic queue rotation.
- **Player Tools & HUD:**
  - Backpack gears: Speed Coil, Gravity Coil, Stage Smoke Remote, Fresh Tender Coconut.
  - Bottom Action Bar: `[🎵 Music]`, `[🎤 Karaoke]`, `[💃 Emotes]`, `[💤 AFK]`, `[🤸 Flop]`, `[🛡️ Admin]`.
  - 100% clean typography with zero square box (tofu) font glitches.
